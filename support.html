What it goes toward

Publishing, research, writing

Academic publishing in archaeology is not free. Open-access fees, conference travel, library access, and the time it takes to write something worth reading all have real costs that institutions don't always cover — especially for independent postdoctoral researchers.

My current focus is on expanding the PAS Schemes Monitor, completing a new theoretical piece on Bronze Age depositional practice, and writing fiction in Portuguese that I intend to publish independently. Any contribution — however small — goes directly toward these.

If you found the Archaeosweeper game fun, the publications useful, or the blog worth reading, this is the most direct way to say so.

Contributions are voluntary and go toward independent research and publishing costs — not payment for any specific product or service.

Other ways to help

No budget? No problem.

The most valuable thing you can do is share the work with someone who might find it useful — a colleague, a student, anyone curious about Bronze Age archaeology, digitalization in public institutions, or just a good browser game.

You can also follow the work on LinkedIn, Bluesky, or Instagram — and leave a comment on the blog if something resonated. That matters too.
